# Railway Disruption Recovery

A regional railway dispatcher must recover a busy bidirectional corridor after a track incident. Trains already have a published timetable and rolling-stock circulation, but the disruption blocks one critical segment for part of the planning horizon. The dispatcher may delay trains, short-turn trains at turnback-capable stations, or cancel trains, while trying to preserve passenger connections and keep rolling stock in a physically possible sequence.

You are supporting a railway control center during a corridor disruption. The
goal is to produce a feasible recovery timetable with low delay, cancellation,
short-turn, missed-connection, and rolling-stock or crew reassignment impact.

Hard operational rules:

- Each train must receive one operational disposition: operate to its original
  destination, turn back early where permitted, or be cancelled.
- A running train must visit the stations along its corridor direction in order.
- A train may turn back early only when that recovery action is authorized for
  it. The train must stop at a turnback-capable station before reaching its
  original destination.
- A cancelled train must not include station events.
- Station event times must be nondecreasing and must respect the minimum dwell
  time at scheduled stops.
- Segment travel between adjacent stations must respect the public running time
  and any disruption window on that segment.
- Trains using the same constrained segment must be separated by the required
  headway.
- Platform numbers must exist at the station, and two trains cannot occupy the
  same platform too close together.
- Station throat routes are limited resources; arrivals and departures through
  the same station side must respect the listed route separation.
- Each assigned rolling-stock unit must have enough passenger capacity for the
  train and may operate only a sequence that is physically chainable with the
  unit's initial location and turn-time requirements.
- Crew assignments must also be physically chainable. A crew can only take a
  train from its current station after its ready/rest time, must be qualified
  for the train direction, and cannot exceed its single-train duty limit.
- The published rolling-stock and crew assignments are preferences rather than
  hard requirements. Substituting another valid unit or crew is allowed but
  incurs a reassignment penalty.
- Protected passenger connections are desirable: if both trains run through the
  transfer station, the receiving train should depart after the required
  transfer time.

Data dictionary:

- `config.json`: planning horizon and objective penalty settings.
- `stations.csv`: corridor order, turnback status, platform counts, and minimum
  station gap.
- `segments.csv`: adjacent station links, running times, headway rules, and
  single-track conflict groups.
- `disruption_windows.csv`: infrastructure blockage windows.
- `trains.csv`: original train plan, direction, demand, priority, rolling-stock
  preference, and allowed recovery actions.
- `rolling_stock.csv`: unit initial locations, ready times, capacities, and
  turn-time requirements.
- `crews.csv`: crew initial positions, rest requirements, qualifications, and
  duty limits.
- `station_throats.csv`: station-side interlocking route separation rules.
- `passenger_connections.csv`: important transfer flows to protect.
- `control_notes.csv`: dispatch notes that may be helpful but are not a
  replacement for the structured tables.

Submission schema:

Write `submissions/solution.json`:

```json
{
  "trains": [
    {
      "train_id": "T01",
      "status": "run",
      "rolling_stock_id": "RS1",
      "crew_id": "CR1",
      "events": [
        {"station_id": "W0", "arrival": 20.0, "departure": 22.0, "platform": 1}
      ]
    }
  ]
}
```

Use `run`, `short_turn`, or `cancel` in the `status` field to encode the three
operational dispositions described above.

Use minutes from the start of the recovery horizon. For a cancelled train,
provide an empty `events` list. For a short-turned train, the final event is the
turnback station.

# Advanced MIP Patterns

## Use when

- The formulation needs logical implications, fixed charges, activation decisions, ordering, no-overlap, disjunctions, min/max, absolute values, tiers, route state, or resource calendars.
- A simple LP/MIP is producing unrealistic behavior because decisions are not linked tightly enough.
- The model is slow or infeasible and the likely cause is weak big-M logic, excessive pairwise variables, or missing state transitions.

## Do not use when

- A continuous LP is sufficient and adding binaries would not represent a real decision.
- The task is better handled by a specialized CP-SAT, constraint programming, nonlinear, or simulation method and the user did not require MIP.
- The user only needs a high-level explanation; do not introduce advanced constructs unless they clarify the actual model.

## General boundary rules

- Add binaries only for real yes/no choices, disjunctions, or nonconvex structure.
- Prefer the tightest formulation that matches the business rule; avoid decorative binaries.
- Use solver-native constructs when they improve clarity and are supported by the target environment.
- If a construct makes the model much slower, compare it with a simpler valid alternative before keeping it.

## Fixed charge and activation

- Use a binary `y` for whether an activity/resource is active.
- Link activity with a tight bound: `x <= U * y`.
- Add lower activation only when the business requires minimum use: `x >= L * y`.
- Charge fixed cost once through `fixed_cost * y`, not per unit.
- Do not force `y = 1` whenever `x = 0` unless the activity is mandatory.
- If there are startup/shutdown costs over time, separate active state from transition state.

## Logical rules

- Prefer solver-native indicator constraints for simple if-then rules when they keep the model clearer.
- If using big-M, derive M from data bounds and keep one M per rule family when possible.
- For either-or choices, use binaries whose sum is exactly one or at most one, matching the business rule.
- Distinguish implication (`A -> B`) from equivalence (`A <-> B`); the reverse direction often needs a separate constraint.
- For conditional capacity, link both activity and resource use to the condition.

## Min, max, absolute value

- For `z = max(a, b)`, add `z >= a`, `z >= b`, and objective pressure if minimizing z. Use binaries only when equality is needed without objective pressure.
- For absolute deviation `d = |x - target|`, add `d >= x - target` and `d >= target - x`.
- For service-level penalties, separate hard feasibility from soft penalty variables.
- For `min`, either transform with signs or add binaries when equality is not enforced by the objective.
- For ratio constraints, cross-multiply only when denominators are constant or safely bounded away from zero.

## Sequencing and no-overlap

- For jobs sharing a resource, use order binary `before[i,j]`.
- Typical pattern:
  - `start[j] >= end[i] - M * (1 - before[i,j])`
  - `start[i] >= end[j] - M * before[i,j]`
- Compute M from the scheduling horizon, not from an arbitrary large number.
- Include setup, cleanup, travel, rest, or turnover time in the correct side of the ordering constraint.
- Create pairwise order variables only for pairs that can share a resource or conflict.
- If time is discrete and the horizon is small, a time-indexed model may be cleaner than pairwise disjunctions.

## Piecewise and tiers

- Use solver-native piecewise-linear features for convex piecewise costs when suitable.
- For nonconvex tiers, use explicit segment variables and segment activation binaries.
- Ensure tier quantities sum to the original activity and each tier has its own upper bound.
- Distinguish incremental tiers from all-units tiers; their formulations and costs differ.
- Check whether discount tiers create nonconvex incentives that require binary selection.

## State and inventory logic

- State variables need initial conditions, transition equations, and terminal requirements if the business specifies them.
- Route or schedule state should be advanced in event order, not checked only row by row.
- Recovery, restock, cleaning, cooldown, maintenance, and rest rules often require cumulative state or last-event logic.
- If state can reset, model the reset trigger and its resource/time cost.

## Debugging advanced models

- First solve a relaxed or smaller instance and inspect whether the intended binaries activate.
- Add named constraints so IIS output points to business rules.
- If the reference solution is infeasible, replay the relevant state transitions manually before changing the evaluator.
- If the LP relaxation is very weak, inspect big-M values, missing lower/upper links, and symmetry.
- If the model is unexpectedly infeasible, test each advanced rule family on a tiny instance with a known feasible solution.

def build_model(data, time_limit):
    horizon = data["horizon"]
    model = Model("railway_recovery")
    model.setParam("limits/gap", 0.0005)
    model.setParam("limits/time", time_limit)
    model.setParam("display/verblevel", 0)

    vars_ = {
        "final": {},
        "cancel": {},
        "active": {},
        "run": {},
        "short": {},
        "visit": {},
        "cont": {},
        "use": {},
        "arr": {},
        "dep": {},
        "finish": {},
        "delay": {},
        "before_window": {},
        "seg_order": {},
        "throat_order": {},
        "miss": {},
        "rs_assign": {},
        "rs_start": {},
        "rs_arc": {},
        "crew_assign": {},
        "crew_start": {},
        "crew_arc": {},
    }

    for train_id, train in data["trains"].items():
        vars_["cancel"][train_id] = model.addVar(vtype="B", name=f"cancel[{train_id}]")
        vars_["active"][train_id] = model.addVar(vtype="B", name=f"active[{train_id}]")
        vars_["run"][train_id] = model.addVar(vtype="B", name=f"run[{train_id}]")
        vars_["short"][train_id] = model.addVar(vtype="B", name=f"short[{train_id}]")
        vars_["finish"][train_id] = model.addVar(lb=0.0, ub=horizon, name=f"finish[{train_id}]")
        vars_["delay"][train_id] = model.addVar(lb=0.0, ub=horizon, name=f"delay[{train_id}]")

        endpoint_set = set(train["feasible_endpoints"])
        for endpoint in train["feasible_endpoints"]:
            vars_["final"][(train_id, endpoint)] = model.addVar(vtype="B", name=f"final[{train_id},{endpoint}]")

        model.addCons(
            quicksum(vars_["final"][(train_id, endpoint)] for endpoint in train["feasible_endpoints"]) + vars_["cancel"][train_id]
            == 1,
            name=f"one_disposition[{train_id}]",
        )
        model.addCons(
            vars_["active"][train_id]
            == quicksum(vars_["final"][(train_id, endpoint)] for endpoint in train["feasible_endpoints"]),
            name=f"active_def[{train_id}]",
        )
        model.addCons(
            vars_["run"][train_id] == vars_["final"][(train_id, train["destination"])],
            name=f"run_def[{train_id}]",
        )
        short_sum = quicksum(
            vars_["final"][(train_id, endpoint)]
            for endpoint in train["feasible_endpoints"]
            if endpoint != train["destination"]
        )
        model.addCons(vars_["short"][train_id] == short_sum, name=f"short_def[{train_id}]")
        if not train["allow_cancel"]:
            model.addCons(vars_["cancel"][train_id] == 0, name=f"cancel_forbidden[{train_id}]")
        if not train["allow_short_turn"]:
            model.addCons(vars_["short"][train_id] == 0, name=f"short_forbidden[{train_id}]")

        for station in train["route"]:
            vars_["visit"][(train_id, station)] = model.addVar(vtype="B", name=f"visit[{train_id},{station}]")
            vars_["cont"][(train_id, station)] = model.addVar(vtype="B", name=f"cont[{train_id},{station}]")
            vars_["arr"][(train_id, station)] = model.addVar(lb=0.0, ub=horizon, name=f"arr[{train_id},{station}]")
            vars_["dep"][(train_id, station)] = model.addVar(lb=0.0, ub=horizon, name=f"dep[{train_id},{station}]")

            visit_expr = quicksum(
                vars_["final"][(train_id, endpoint)]
                for endpoint in train["feasible_endpoints"]
                if train["route_pos"][station] <= train["route_pos"][endpoint]
            )
            cont_expr = quicksum(
                vars_["final"][(train_id, endpoint)]
                for endpoint in train["feasible_endpoints"]
                if train["route_pos"][station] < train["route_pos"][endpoint]
            )
            model.addCons(vars_["visit"][(train_id, station)] == visit_expr, name=f"visit_def[{train_id},{station}]")
            model.addCons(vars_["cont"][(train_id, station)] == cont_expr, name=f"cont_def[{train_id},{station}]")
            model.addCons(vars_["arr"][(train_id, station)] <= horizon * vars_["visit"][(train_id, station)], name=f"arr_act[{train_id},{station}]")
            model.addCons(vars_["dep"][(train_id, station)] <= horizon * vars_["visit"][(train_id, station)], name=f"dep_act[{train_id},{station}]")

        origin = train["origin"]
        model.addCons(vars_["arr"][(train_id, origin)] == vars_["dep"][(train_id, origin)], name=f"origin_equal[{train_id}]")

            name=f"origin_lb[{train_id}]",

                vars_["dep"][(train_id, station)] >= vars_["arr"][(train_id, station)] + train["min_dwell"] * vars_["cont"][(train_id, station)],
                name=f"dwell_lb[{train_id},{station}]",

            model.addCons(

                name=f"dwell_ub[{train_id},{station}]",

        for idx, seg_id in enumerate(train["segment_sequence"]):
            entry_station = train["route"][idx]

            vars_["use"][(train_id, seg_id)] = model.addVar(vtype="B", name=f"use[{train_id},{seg_id}]")
            use_expr = quicksum(
                vars_["final"][(train_id, endpoint)]
                for endpoint in train["feasible_endpoints"]
                if train["route_pos"][endpoint] >= idx + 1

                vars_["arr"][(train_id, exit_station)] >= vars_["dep"][(train_id, entry_station)] + run_minutes - horizon * (1 - vars_["use"][(train_id, seg_id)]),

                vars_["arr"][(train_id, exit_station)] <= vars_["dep"][(train_id, entry_station)] + run_minutes + horizon * (1 - vars_["use"][(train_id, seg_id)]),
                name=f"run_ub[{train_id},{seg_id}]",
            )

            for w_idx, window in enumerate(data["disruptions_by_segment"].get(seg_id, [])):
                before = model.addVar(vtype="B", name=f"before_window[{train_id},{seg_id},{w_idx}]")
                vars_["before_window"][(train_id, seg_id, w_idx)] = before
                model.addCons(
                    vars_["arr"][(train_id, exit_station)] <= window["start"] + horizon * (1 - before) + horizon * (1 - vars_["use"][(train_id, seg_id)]),
                    name=f"window_before[{train_id},{seg_id},{w_idx}]",

                model.addCons(
                    vars_["dep"][(train_id, entry_station)] >= window["end"] - horizon * before - horizon * (1 - vars_["use"][(train_id, seg_id)]),
                    name=f"window_after[{train_id},{seg_id},{w_idx}]",
                )

        for endpoint in train["feasible_endpoints"]:
            model.addCons(
                vars_["finish"][train_id] >= vars_["arr"][(train_id, endpoint)] - horizon * (1 - vars_["final"][(train_id, endpoint)]),
                name=f"finish_lb[{train_id},{endpoint}]",
            )
            model.addCons(
                vars_["finish"][train_id] <= vars_["arr"][(train_id, endpoint)] + horizon * (1 - vars_["final"][(train_id, endpoint)]),
                name=f"finish_ub[{train_id},{endpoint}]",
            )

        planned_terminal = quicksum(
            train["planned_arrival"][endpoint] * vars_["final"][(train_id, endpoint)]
            for endpoint in train["feasible_endpoints"]
        )
        model.addCons(vars_["delay"][train_id] >= vars_["finish"][train_id] - planned_terminal, name=f"delay_def[{train_id}]")
        model.addCons(vars_["delay"][train_id] <= horizon * vars_["active"][train_id], name=f"delay_act[{train_id}]")

    for connection in data["connections"]:
        cid = connection["id"]
        from_train = connection["from_train"]
        to_train = connection["to_train"]
        station = connection["station_id"]
        vars_["miss"][cid] = model.addVar(vtype="B", name=f"miss[{cid}]")

        model.addCons(vars_["miss"][cid] <= vars_["visit"][(to_train, station)], name=f"miss_to[{cid}]")

            - horizon * vars_["miss"][cid]

        west_trains.sort()

                    - horizon * (2 - vars_["use"][(t1, seg_id)] - vars_["use"][(t2, seg_id)]),

                    - horizon * (2 - vars_["use"][(t1, seg_id)] - vars_["use"][(t2, seg_id)]),

                    - horizon * order
