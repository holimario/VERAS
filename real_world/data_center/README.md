# Data-Center Placement

The current manuscript uses the **2025-08-10 retrospective comparison**.
Archived VERAS has a maximum directional peak of **1,110.602830 Gbps**;
the later configuration-matched native Codex arm has **1,238.625115 Gbps**.
This is a descriptive 10.336% lower peak for the archived VERAS candidate.
Both are strictly feasible and generated byte-identically from public inputs
within 120 seconds. The archived VERAS/native runs share public input and evaluator
hashes, model/effort, timeout scale, and configured agent cap, but differ in
actual agent time and tokens. The archived candidate had been selected after earlier August 10
research feedback; the comparison was not jointly precommitted.

A fresh precommitted same-prompt VERAS repeat records **1,567.302579 Gbps**
on August 10 and reverses the archived/native ordering. All three outcomes
are retained, not selected by the sign of the comparison.
